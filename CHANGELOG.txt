RexUI 3.9
---------
- Introduced the integrated graphics optimizer and its three quality profiles.
- Graphics profiles aligned with the ElvUI Retail performance guide updated for
  WoW 12.0/12.0.1: separate raid graphics, 100% render scale, essential raid
  spell density, projected textures enabled, and low raid environment detail.
- Hardware-specific Graphics API and low-latency settings are deliberately left
  to the user instead of being changed blindly.
- Graphics profile labels clarified as Optimal for Raid & M+, Balanced, and Best
  Graphics; the settings page now explains the currently selected profile.
